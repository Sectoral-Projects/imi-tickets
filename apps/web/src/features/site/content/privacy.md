## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **{{name}}** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our Discord ticket bot or staff website (the "Service"), including when you:

- open or participate in a ticket;
- interact with ticket messages, attachments, or reactions;
- sign in to the staff website; or
- visit {{url}} or another page that links to this policy.

If you have questions about this policy or your information, contact us at {{contact}}.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Ticket participants

When you interact with the bot, we collect the information needed to operate and preserve the ticket. This may include:

- your Discord user ID, username, global display name, nickname, avatar, roles, and server membership;
- ticket messages, edits, staff notes, attachments, attachment links and metadata, reactions, and timestamps;
- ticket participants, channel and message IDs, ticket status, tags, and staff actions; and
- records of blocked users or servers and incomplete ticket-opening requests.

Any interaction with the bot may create a record. This includes opening or joining a ticket, sending or editing a message, uploading an attachment, and adding or removing a reaction.

### 2.2 Staff sign-in

Staff sign in through Discord. We request their Discord identity and the servers they share with the bot so we can authenticate them and determine which parts of the Service they may access. We may store authentication tokens supplied by Discord, session records, IP addresses, and browser or device information used during sign-in. We do not ask for a separate email address for login.

## 3. How we use information

We use this information to:

- open, route, relay, manage, and close tickets;
- display ticket history and create transcripts;
- authenticate staff and enforce server-specific permissions;
- support moderation features such as blocks, audit records, and ticket status history; and
- maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

## 4. How information is shared

The Service operates through Discord. Ticket messages and related information are therefore sent to and stored by Discord as part of normal bot operation. Discord processes that information under its [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

Our hosting, network, or backup providers may process information on our behalf when that is necessary to run the Service. The providers involved depend on how we host this installation.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the Service or its users, or with your direction or consent. We do not sell personal information. The Service does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or payment processing.

## 5. Storage and retention

We store Service data in a SQLite database on the system where we host the Service. Discord retains messages, attachments, and other information sent through its platform according to Discord's own practices.

We keep ticket and account information while it is needed to operate the Service, maintain ticket records, resolve disputes, enforce our rules, or meet legal obligations. We may delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

## 6. Access, export, and deletion requests

You may ask us for a copy of information associated with your Discord account or ask us to delete or anonymize it. The Service does not provide members with a self-service deletion button. Submit a request through {{contact}} or by opening another ticket asking about data deletion.

We may ask you to verify control of the relevant Discord account before completing a request. Some information may be retained when required by law or when deletion would affect records we must keep for security, dispute resolution, or enforcement.

## 7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No storage or transmission system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Service, our practices, or applicable requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top identifies the current version.
